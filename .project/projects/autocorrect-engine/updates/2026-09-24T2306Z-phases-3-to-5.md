# Progress Update

## Completed
- Phases 0 to 5 (T-001 to T-019) are built, tested and committed on `t3code/improve-autocorrect`; T-020 is deferred by design. The benchmark runs in the unit tests (550 tests) and fails the build if any passed gate regresses.
- Against the spec's final targets (tap-noise sets with tap positions, Normal strength): typos fixed EN 81.5% (target 75), NL 79.7% (72), NL on NL+EN 75.6% (65); precision 98.5 to 99.6% on every set (98); intended word first 92.4% EN and 92.1% NL (92). Without tap positions (glide, hardware keyboards): 70.4%, 69.3% and 62.2%. Numbers from `research/benchmark-phase5-t019.md`.
- Real-world misspellings: EN curated 82% (70), NL 86 to 90% (85), none wrong. Clean text: at most 0.13 false corrections per 1,000 words (0.3). Out-of-dictionary words: 1 of 313 on the NL+EN set, 2 of 313 on the EN set (target 1; `Thijs` -> This and `standup` -> stand up).
- Context: typos in held-out sentences fixed EN 78%, NL 69% (84% and 77% with taps); real-word errors with the intended word first EN 71%, NL 51% (target 60; the Dutch misses need the next word, T-020). Next-word predictions contain the actual next word 29% (EN) and 24% (NL) of the time on held-out sentences, 34% on hand-written ones, against 5 to 10% before.
- Legacy baseline for comparison: 0% of typos corrected at default settings.
- Speed on the emulator (debug build, all phases): the on-the-spot autocorrect decision on space took 4 ms at p50 and 9 ms at p95 (max 14 ms, 26 decisions while typing four sentences on EN and NL+EN); bigram models load in 0.1 to 0.2 s per language off the main thread.
- Every phase had an independent read-only review; all confirmed defects were fixed with regression tests (crash on "İ", taps from the wrong finger, a stale suggestion cache for "I", a bigram parse failure that dropped the word list, Tatoeba names in predictions, an undo that stayed armed and could replace a correctly typed word, the undo cursor, lowercase "i" in splits, learned-word counts after a restart, no length cap on the two-edit lookup). Run-together pairs are fixed 75.8% (EN) and 67.4% (NL) after the stricter split threshold.
